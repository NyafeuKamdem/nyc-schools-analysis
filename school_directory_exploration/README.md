# School Directory Exploration

## 🎯 Objective
Transitioned from spreadsheets to Python. I used Pandas in a Jupyter Notebook to clean the NYC High School Directory dataset and perform a comparative analysis of school structures across the five boroughs.

## 🛠️ Process
- **Data Cleaning:** Automated column standardization (lowercase, underscores, special character removal) using Python.
- **Geographic Filtering:** Isolated Brooklyn-specific data to audit Grade 9 entry points and unique school counts.
- **Aggregation:** Grouped data by borough to compare student population density and grade span statistics.
- **Visualization:** Generated bar charts to visualize school distribution across NYC.

## 📈 Key Findings
- **Brooklyn Entry Points:** Analysis confirmed that Grade 9 is the universal entry point for Brooklyn high schools. 99.17% of these schools extend through Grade 12.
- **The Staten Island Model:** Staten Island stands out with a significantly higher student density (avg. 1,847 students per school). 
- **Small-School Diversity:** Unlike other boroughs, Staten Island lacks "small school" diversity; its minimum school size is higher than the median of almost every other borough, indicating a focus on high-capacity hubs rather than specialized smaller institutions.
